## Naming and Positioning Rules

- AI never appears in offer names (the workshop is the one grandfathered exception). AI is the delivery method, never the marketing label.
- Positioning is identity-first: build from the inside out, excavation not invention, AI amplifies who you already are.
- Two avatars anchor all copy: The Dreamer (credentialed expert about to build) and The Rebuilder (established founder needing realignment).
- No hustle language, no hype, calm confidence over urgency.

## Writing Rules (apply to all copy)

No em dashes ever. No emojis. Never begin a sentence with "And." Warm and authoritative. Short punchy sentences mixed with longer ones for depth. Psychology-informed, smart yet down-to-earth.
