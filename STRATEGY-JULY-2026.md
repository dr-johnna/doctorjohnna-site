## The strategy (memorize this, ignore everything else)

**Teach weekly. Invite daily. Launch quarterly.**

1. **Teach weekly.** One cornerstone piece per week (a podcast episode or a live teach in the Studio). Your existing repurposing skills turn it into the whole week's social content. You already have this machinery. Point it at the ladder.
2. **Invite daily.** Every email and every post ends with one next step on the ladder. One offer per day, minimum. Free content with no invitation is a donation. This is the single biggest behavior change, and it costs nothing.
3. **Launch quarterly.** Four Accelerator cohorts a year, each opened by the free masterclass. Between launches, the $37 workshop replay sells on autopilot and feeds the Studio.

That is Hormozi's volume, Golden's offer-every-day, and Cochrane's email engine in one sentence. Nothing else is required. Everything below is just this strategy applied to July.

**One metric per week: invitations sent.** Emails to the list, DMs, personal notes, posts with a CTA. Target 50 per week minimum. Revenue follows invitations, nowhere else.
